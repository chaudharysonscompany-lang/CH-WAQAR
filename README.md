# CH-WAQAR

### Property solutions for buying, selling, building, and maintaining real estate

We help people find suitable residential and commercial properties, make informed property decisions, and care for buildings beyond the sale. Our work spans property search and transactions, investment guidance, construction coordination, and ongoing maintenance.

## What we do

- **Buy and sell property** — houses, apartments, buildings, and commercial plots.
- **Find the right fit** — property selection, listings, site visits, and tours tailored to customer needs.
- **Support investment decisions** — property consultation, valuation guidance, market-price estimates, and negotiation assistance.
- **Plan and build** — house construction, commercial development, architectural planning, material guidance, and project supervision.
- **Maintain buildings** — arrange repairs and help keep properties in good condition.
- **Explore development opportunities** — learn about residential and commercial projects, their facilities, and available payment options.

## How we work

1. **Understand your goals** — the property type, budget, location, and intended use.
2. **Explore suitable options** — review available listings and arrange property visits where appropriate.
3. **Make an informed choice** — discuss valuation, investment considerations, and negotiation.
4. **Support the next steps** — from construction and project coordination to maintenance and after-sales assistance.

## Residential and commercial projects

Depending on the project, facilities may include gated-community security, utilities, roads and streetlights, parks, mosques or community centers, and commercial areas. Availability varies by development.
